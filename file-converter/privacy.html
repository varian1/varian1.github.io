FileConverter Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

In short: your images and audio files are converted on your device and are never uploaded. There is no account and no FileConverter server, so we never receive your files. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

FileConverter ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play page ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimiteds@gmail.com.

Your files stay on your device

  • Picking files. You choose images and audio files with the system file browser, so the app only sees the files you pick. It never asks for access to all your photos, music or storage.
  • Conversion runs entirely on your device, using Android's own image and audio decoders and a built-in MP3 encoder. Your files, their contents and their names are not uploaded.
  • File details. The app reads details such as file name, size, image dimensions, audio duration, bitrate and tags (title, artist, album) only to show them on screen and to name the converted file.
  • Photo details (metadata). When converting to JPG you can keep or remove the photo's details (for example camera model, date and, if the photo has it, location). If you keep them, they stay inside the converted file and travel with it when you share it.
  • Converted files are saved in the app's own folder on your device. We have no copy. The app deletes them when you start a new batch, when you open the app again, and when you uninstall it.
  • Sharing and saving. Converted files leave the app only when you tap Share (to the app or person you pick) or Save to folder (to a folder you pick on your device).
  • Backups. The app opts out of Android system backup, so your files and settings are not copied to a cloud backup by the app.
  • No analytics. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own and keeps no log of your files.

Data collected by Google AdMob

We don't operate any servers. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance).

AdMob does not receive your images, audio files or converted files. Data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your choice before any ad loads, and you can change it any time with the Ad privacy choices link under the ad banner. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Converted files stay in the app's folder on your device until you start a new batch, open the app again, clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → FileConverter → Storage) or uninstall the app. Copies you saved with Save to folder stay in that folder until you delete them. Your original files are never changed or deleted. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you. For ad data held by Google, use your device's ad settings and Google's My Ad Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to Google for data held by AdMob.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
