Battery Monitor: Charge Log Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

In short: there is no account and no server of ours. Your battery readings and charging history stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Premium turns ads off.

Who we are

Battery Monitor: Charge Log ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play page ("we"). For any privacy question, email kitvarastudio@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Battery history. While the app is open, and about every 15 minutes in the background, it saves the time, battery level, temperature, voltage and whether the phone is plugged in. From these it records charging sessions (start and end time and level, power source and peak temperature). This is kept in a database in the app's private storage for up to 90 days. You can delete all of it in Settings.
  • Purchase status. Whether Premium is unlocked is stored in the app's private storage.
  • No backups. The app turns off Android's automatic app backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • The battery values come from Android's public battery information, which needs no permission. The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and does not need an account.

Data collected by services in the app

Google AdMob (free version)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example performance and crash data).

A banner ad appears at the bottom of the screen. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it later under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Google settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. With Premium, no ads are shown and the ads SDK is not started.

Google Play Billing (Premium)

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether you own Premium; we never see your payment details, name or email. See Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not run analytics, crash reporting or advertising of our own, do not sell your data, and do not build a profile of you. Your battery history is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your battery history stays on your device for up to 90 days, or until you delete it in Settings or uninstall the app. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you. For data held by Google, use the privacy tools linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to Google for data held by its services.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
