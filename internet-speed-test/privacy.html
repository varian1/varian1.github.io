Internet Speed Test & Ping Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

In short: there is no account and no server of ours. Your test results and settings stay on your device. To measure your speed, the app exchanges test data with a speed test server (Cloudflare), which sees your IP address like any website you visit. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Premium turns ads off.

Who we are

Internet Speed Test & Ping ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play page ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimiteds@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Test history. After each test the app saves the date and time, download and upload speed, ping, jitter and the connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular) in a database in the app's private storage. You can delete one result or clear all in the app.
  • Settings and purchase status. Your settings (such as monitor interval) and whether Premium is unlocked are stored in the app's private storage.
  • No backups. The app turns off Android's automatic app backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, does not read your Wi-Fi network name, and does not need an account.

The speed test server

A test sends and receives data over your current connection. The app uses the public speed test service at speed.cloudflare.com, operated by Cloudflare, Inc. For the app to work, that server necessarily receives your IP address and the test traffic (random data that does not contain your information), as any website does. We do not receive this data and we do not add identifiers to it. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Tests use mobile data when you are not on Wi-Fi, which can count against your data plan.

The app checks whether your device is online and on Wi-Fi or cellular, to enable the Start button and label your results. This stays on the device.

Data collected by services in the app

Google AdMob (free version)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example performance and crash data).

Ads appear on the History, Insights and Settings screens, not during a test. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Google settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. With Premium, no ads are shown.

Google Play Billing (Premium)

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether you own Premium; we never see your payment details, name or email. See Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not run analytics, crash reporting or advertising of our own, do not sell your data, and do not build a profile of you. We receive none of your test results.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your history and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you. For data held by Cloudflare or Google, use their privacy tools linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to the right provider for data held by its services.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
