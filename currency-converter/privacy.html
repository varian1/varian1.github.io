Currency Converter Offline Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

In short: the amounts you type and the currencies you save stay on your device. There is no account and no server of ours that receives your data. To get exchange rates, the app connects to the rate source you choose, and the free app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

Currency Converter Offline ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play page ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimited@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your choices. Saved currencies and their order, the amount and currency you last used, theme, language and the selected rate source are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Downloaded rates. The last exchange rates the app downloaded are kept on your device so the app works offline.
  • Server status results. The latest results of the checks described below are kept on your device.
  • Rating reminder. The app counts, on your device, on how many days you used it, to decide when to ask you to rate it once.
  • Backups. On Android, this app data may be included in your Android (Google) backup if you have backups turned on, like most apps' data. We cannot see it.
  • The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location, and it has no account, login or analytics of its own.

Connections the app makes

Exchange-rate sources

To download rates, the app connects over HTTPS to the source selected in Settings:

  • Currency API (default) through the jsDelivr content network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), with a fallback on Cloudflare Pages (currency-api.pages.dev);
  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev);
  • ExchangeRate-API Open Access (open.er-api.com).

The app sends no account, advertising ID or other identifier in these requests, but like any website the service you select can see your IP address and the time of the request. Each service has its own privacy practices. Exchange rates are reference rates and may differ from the rates offered by banks or exchange services.

Server status checks

Settings has a "Server status" section. Only when you tap Check or Check all, the app requests a small status file hosted by the developer for the services named there. These requests send no personal data, and the host can see your IP address like any web server.

Google Play in-app review

After you have used the app on a few different days, it may ask you once to rate it using Google Play's in-app review. Google handles that dialog and any rating you give under its own privacy policy.

Data collected by Google AdMob

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example crashes and performance).

Data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it later in Settings → Ad privacy choices, which appears where such a choice applies. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your amounts, saved currencies and the other data listed above under "What stays on your device" are not sent to Google or to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you. For ad data held by Google, use your device's ad settings and Google's My Ad Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to Google or to the rate service you selected for data they hold.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
