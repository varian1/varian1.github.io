Photo & Image Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective [publication date]

In short: your photos stay on your device. Compressing, resizing, cropping, converting, collages and background removal all run on your phone. There is no account and no Photo & Image Toolkit server, so we never receive your photos. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Premium turns the ad SDK off completely.

Who we are

Photo & Image Toolkit ("the app") is published by StartFirst ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimiteds@gmail.com.

Your photos stay on your device

  • Choosing photos. The app uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you pick. It never asks to read your whole photo library.
  • Processing (compression, resizing, cropping, format conversion, collages and background removal) runs on your device. Background removal uses Google ML Kit on Android and Apple Vision on iPhone and iPad. Your photos are not uploaded.
  • Location and camera details. Edited copies are saved without the original photo's metadata (such as GPS location and camera details).
  • Saving. Edited copies are written to your photo library only when you tap Save. On iPhone and iPad the app asks only for permission to add photos. Your originals are never changed.
  • Temporary files. While you work, edited copies are kept in the app's private cache on your device; the system may clear it at any time.
  • Backups. On Android the app opts out of system backup. On iPhone and iPad, app data can be included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have turned those on, like other apps' data.
  • Sharing. Images leave the app only when you tap Share, and only to the app or person you pick.

Data collected by services in the app

We don't operate any servers or analytics. The app includes these third-party components:

Google AdMob (free version only)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance).

Data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

With Premium, the ad SDK is never started, so none of this ad data is collected.

RevenueCat (purchases)

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Google Play or the App Store, and managed through RevenueCat, Inc. To confirm what you have bought, RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase history for this app (product, price, currency, date, store transaction ID) and basic device information (such as OS version, app version, country and IP address). We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Google ML Kit (Android)

Background removal runs on your device. The model is delivered by Google Play services. ML Kit sends Google basic usage and performance metrics (device model and OS version, app name and version, a per-installation identifier that does not identify you, processing time and error codes) for diagnostics. It does not send your photos. See ML Kit's terms.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Photos you save stay in your photo library until you delete them there. Uninstalling the app removes its cache and settings. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you. For ad data held by Google, use your device's ad settings and Google's My Ad Center. For purchase records, contact us and we will help you reach RevenueCat.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to Google or RevenueCat for data held by their services.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
