Virtual Buttons Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: Virtual Buttons has no account and no server, so we never receive your data. The Accessibility service only presses the buttons you tap; it does not read your screen or what you type. Screen recordings and screenshots stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

Virtual Buttons ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play page ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimiteds@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Accessibility service. When you turn it on, the app uses it only to perform the button you tap (Home, Back, Recent apps, notifications, Quick Settings, power menu, lock screen, screenshot) and to show the floating button over other apps. It is set up so it cannot read window content, and it does not see what is on your screen or what you type. Nothing is collected or shared through it.
  • Screen recordings start only after you confirm Android's screen-capture prompt, and a notification with a Stop button shows while recording. Videos have no sound (the app does not use the microphone) and are saved on your phone: in Movies/Virtual Buttons on Android 10 and newer, or in the app's own folder on Android 9 (removed when you uninstall the app). The app never uploads them.
  • Screenshots are taken by Android itself and saved where Android saves your screenshots.
  • Notifications permission is used only for the recording notification with its Stop button.
  • Settings (floating button layout, size, colour and position, whether you accepted the accessibility notice, whether Premium is unlocked, and the days the buttons were used, so the app can ask for a rating at a good moment) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Backups. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your own Google backup so they come back on a new phone. We cannot access that backup.

Data collected by Google services in the app

We don't run any servers or analytics. The app includes these Google components, which send some data to Google:

Google AdMob (free version)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance).

Data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it any time in Premium & info → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

With Premium, the app does not ask for ad consent or request any ads.

Google Play

Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Premium is owned. Rating requests use Google Play's in-app review, and Google decides whether the prompt appears.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings stay on your phone until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Recordings in Movies stay until you delete them, like your other videos. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export or delete it for you. For ad data held by Google, use your device's ad settings and Google's My Ad Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to Google for data held by its services.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
