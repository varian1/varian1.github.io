All-in-One Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

In short: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data. Every conversion runs on your device, and there is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

Who we are

All-in-One Unit Converter ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play and App Store pages ("we"). For any privacy question, email support@example.com.

What stays on your device

  • Conversions are calculated on your device. The values you type are never sent anywhere.
  • Favorites, history and settings (your starred categories, the results you copied, and your light/dark choice) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. We have no copy.
  • Clipboard. When you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device's clipboard, where other apps you use can read it, as with any copied text.
  • Backups. On Android the app opts out of system backup. On iPhone and iPad, app data can be included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have turned those on, like other apps' data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, makes no network requests of its own, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software. We receive no information about you or how you use the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can remove favorites by un-starring them, delete history with History → Clear all, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example ads or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
