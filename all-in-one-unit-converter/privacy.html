All-in-One Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: every conversion runs on your device, and there is no account, no server of ours and no analytics. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below. An optional one-time purchase removes the ads. We do not collect or receive personal data ourselves.

Who we are

All-in-One Unit Converter ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play and App Store pages ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimiteds@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Conversions are calculated on your device. The values you type are never sent anywhere.
  • Favorites, history and settings (your starred categories, the results you copied, and your light/dark choice) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. We have no copy.
  • Clipboard. When you tap Copy, the result is placed on your device's clipboard, where other apps you use can read it, as with any copied text.
  • Backups. On Android the app opts out of system backup. On iPhone and iPad, app data can be included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have turned those on, like other apps' data.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. The app has no account and no server of ours, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. The values you convert, your favorites and your history are never sent anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads on the Browse and History screens using Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app sends Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as ad load errors.

This information is collected and used by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone and iPad the app does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier is not shared with Google. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (rating PG).

Premium purchase

Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes all ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies; we never see your payment details. The app asks the store whether your account owns Premium and keeps only a yes/no flag on your device. Premium users never see ads, and the app does not start the AdMob software for them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not collect personal information ourselves from anyone.

Deleting your data

You can remove favorites by un-starring them, delete history with History → Clear all, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To control data held by Google for ads, use your advertising ID settings above or My Ad Center.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
