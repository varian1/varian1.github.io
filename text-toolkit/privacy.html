Text Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: the text you type or paste is processed on your device and is never sent to us. There is no account and no Text Toolkit server. The app does not save your text: it is gone when the app is closed. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

Text Toolkit ("the app") is published by the developer shown on its Google Play and App Store pages ("we"). For any privacy question, email maximumunlimiteds@gmail.com.

Your text stays on your device

  • Processing. Counting, case conversion, cleaning, sorting, find and replace, reversing, encoding and decoding, JSON and Markdown formatting and text comparison all run on your device. Your text is never uploaded.
  • No storage. The app keeps your text only in memory while it is open. It does not save it to files, a database or the cloud. On Android the app also opts out of system backup.
  • Clipboard. The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste, and writes to it only when you tap Copy or Copy Diff.
  • Sharing. Text leaves the app only when you tap Share, and only to the app or person you pick in your device's share sheet.
  • No permissions to your content. The app does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Data collected by Google AdMob

We don't operate any servers or analytics. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance).

The ad SDK does not receive the text you type or paste. Data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it any time with the Ad privacy choices link on the app's main screen. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because the app does not save your text and we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. Closing or uninstalling the app removes everything it had in memory. For ad data held by Google, use your device's ad settings and Google's My Ad Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data. Contact us at the address above and we will help, including by pointing you to Google for data held by its services.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
